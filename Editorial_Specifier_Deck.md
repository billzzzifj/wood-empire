### Coastal Humidity & Substrate Advisory
**SUBSTRATE ARCHITECTURE & ENVIRONMENTAL DURABILITY**

Lagos coastal zones (Victoria Island, Ikoyi, Lekki) exhibit extreme diurnal humidity fluctuations and high salinity. To ensure architectural integrity and prevent carcass swelling, this specification dictates the utilization of **18mm High-Moisture Resistant (HMR) Green-Core MDF** or **Treated Marine Plywood**.

These substrates provide superior dimensional stability compared to standard MFC, acting as a structural shield against moisture ingress. The specified natural wood veneer is sealed with a robust **2K Matte Polyurethane (PU) spray lacquer**, providing a deeply tactile, water-repellent facade while preserving the authentic wood grain.

### Pre-Installation Site Readiness Checklist

**SITE PREPARATION PROTOCOL**
To ensure pristine installation and zero contamination of millwork, the following site conditions must be met prior to our mobilization:

- [ ] **Wet Works Completion:** All plastering, screeding, and painting in the vicinity must be fully cured and dry.
- [ ] **Climate Stabilization:** Air conditioning systems must be active to stabilize ambient humidity and temperature, mitigating timber acclimatization shock.
- [ ] **Electrical Integration:** Concealed electrical conduit points and junction boxes for integrated LED millwork lighting must be actively wired and terminated correctly.
- [ ] **Clear Access:** Installation zones must be entirely cleared of debris, other tradesmen, and existing fixtures to allow unobstructed laser-leveling and joinery alignment.
