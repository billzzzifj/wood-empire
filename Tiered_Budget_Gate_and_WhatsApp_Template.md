## Tiered Budget Gate & Routing Logic

### Route 1: Private Luxury & Commercial Fit-Out
**Trigger:** Verified Budget >= ₦3,500,000 OR Full-Scope Project.
**Action:**
- Unlock Priority Site Laser-Measurement & Material Sample Presentation booking.
- Option for ₦50,000–₦100,000 deductible consultation fee or direct VIP slot.
- Generate a structured WhatsApp API booking card for Wood Empire Lagos's Principal Estimator.
- Issue the full PDF Specifier Dossier.

### Route 2: Architect / Interior Designer / Developer Partner
**Trigger:** B2B Trade Portal Selection.
**Action:**
- Unlock `.DWG` / `.SKP` CAD joinery profiles.
- Offer physical timber/board swatch box delivery to their studio.
- Unlock volume developer rate cards for multi-unit short-lets or residential estates.

### Route 3: Budget Variance Detected
**Trigger:** Selected Spec > Stated Budget (and budget < ₦3,500,000).
**Action:**
- Trigger an instant **"Apply Value-Engineering Optimization"** button.
- Automatically swaps Tier 3 Solid Teak + Blum Hardware down to Tier 1 Egger Woodgrain MFC + DTC Soft-Close while preserving exact dimensions.
- Displays immediate Naira savings to rescue the sale without manual back-and-forth.
